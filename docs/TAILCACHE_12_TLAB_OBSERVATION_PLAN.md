## Hypothesis and observation

JMH and the every-operation loop execute at different achieved rates and allocate different infrastructure data between iterations. TLAB sizing/refill behavior may therefore differ even when cache bytes per operation match. A refill-cost frequency near0.1% can affect p99.9. This is an unproven hypothesis; a logging correlation alone cannot establish causality or identify a particular timed refill.

Enable only -Xlog:gc+tlab=debug:stdout:timenanos,pid,tid,level,tags through an explicit diagnostic JAVA_TOOL_OPTIONS override. Unlike trace logging, debug publishes aggregate TLAB totals once per GC rather than every refill. Retain all parent/worker output and select only the actual worker PID from its immutable marker when analysing. Keep the ordinary GC file/window evidence for matching GC IDs/timestamps. The OpenJDK21 source describes aggregate allocating threads,total/max refill counts,slow allocations and waste: https://github.com/openjdk/jdk21u/blob/master/src/hotspot/share/gc/shared/threadLocalAllocBuffer.cpp . These are per-collection statistics, not lifetime monotone counters or per-thread TLAB sizes. Do not infer exact live heap, a worker-specific buffer size or allocation latency from the aggregate alone.
