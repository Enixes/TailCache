## Verdict

The checked arithmetic and retained evidence support the scoped descriptive results. I found no numerical discrepancy in this pass. The study is potentially defensible as an exploratory empirical preprint after author review and artifact preparation. It does **not** establish a causal off-heap benefit, a universal crossover threshold, or a matched payload-dependent shift in that threshold. As a claim to a substantial general systems contribution, it still needs major conceptual and validation work; correct plots alone do not establish novelty or generality.

The most useful result remains the repeated scheduled-response p99.9 endpoint reversal: Caffeine is lower at 0 MiB, Chronicle at 208 MiB, and 104 MiB is mixed. Extreme-response advantages already exist at baseline. Original ordinary-service RQ1 remains negative/unresolved in the tested regimes, and the scheduled-response threshold-shift part of RQ2 remains unanswered.

## Research judgment

**Soundness.** Per-fork distributions, source separation, paired directions and preserved contrary/warning-bearing observations are strengths. Three primary pairs and one pair per additional seed are descriptive replication. The endpoint reversal survives this arithmetic audit; its population probability, precise location and monotonicity do not follow from it.

**Mechanism.** Heap occupancy, allocation and pause overlap are consistent with known tradeoffs. They do not identify which individual latency event a GC caused, exclude scheduler/recorder effects, or separate library design from storage placement. A pause between calls may enter gap/response but neither call's service boundary. Larger response tails can represent debt from the same stall, rather than many independent pauses.

**Extreme tails.** A fresh fork has only about 15 nominal observations beyond p99.999, and the one-second scheduled controls have fewer than one nominal observation in that tail. Those values are empirical order statistics with HDR quantization and temporal correlation, not reliable rare-event risk estimates or hard latency guarantees. Higher percentile does not imply Chronicle must win.

**Novelty and significance.** The targeted prior-work review already identifies cache/GC and materialization tradeoffs and a closely related practitioner comparison. The potentially useful contribution is the boundary-sensitive comparison, unresolved middle, per-fork full-tail visibility and heap/RSS distinction. This review adds no new literature search and makes no claim of exhaustive novelty validation. A domain reviewer should assess whether that narrow contribution merits the intended venue before stronger publication claims are made.

**What is needed next.** Finish independent human/contribution review, author revisions, clean harness build/smoke reproduction, raw-evidence backup/release selection, licensing, typesetting and final numerical/citation/layout checks. A longer/nonrepeating trace, additional loads, equal-total-memory intervention, independent recorder or second host would address specific scientific limits as separately planned extensions. This pass does not make a large new campaign a hidden prerequisite for a carefully scoped exploratory draft.
