# Service and response follow-up: completed exploratory results

**17 November 2026.** This is a completed, bounded exploratory extension, not a retrospectively qualified version of the original NO_GO experiment. [Current state](../TAILCACHE_CURRENT_STATE.md) is the entry point; [manuscript](../preprint/MANUSCRIPT.md), [review](FINAL_REVIEW_20261117.md), [claim map](CLAIMS_20261117.md) and [interactive explorer](../TAILCACHE_RESULTS_EXPLORER.html#service-followup) give interpretation and limits.

## Research answers

**RQ1:** No robust, fixed-payload forward pressure crossover in ordinary service p99/p99.9 was established. Small-value wins in the every-operation recorder already exist at baseline, depend on Caffeine policy, and are not consistently reproduced in the separate JMH regime. At 4 KiB and 8 KiB, increasing tested pressure did not improve Chronicle's primary service ranking. This is a useful negative result within the tested range, not proof that a crossover cannot exist elsewhere.

**RQ2:** Payload changes the observed ordering, but it does not yield a portable crossover threshold. The primary service replication covers 512 B, 1 KiB, 2 KiB and 4 KiB at the same 0/208 MiB ballast, plus separate high-pressure discovery and seed holdouts. The matched 256 B/4 KiB paced experiment at 0/208/336 MiB favors Chronicle in response p99.9 already at baseline in its long-trace regime. Consequently it does not estimate movement of a shared pressure-emergent response threshold.

The earlier 10 November 4 KiB short-trace response reversal between 0 and 208 MiB remains valid within its own scope; 104 MiB remains mixed. It is not invariant to trace pattern. No old data or original acceptance was replaced.

### Policy and harness sensitivity

At 512 B baseline the policy cohort gives Ch/C service p99.9 ratios 0.750/0.730 against bounded-default Caffeine, 0.611/0.635 against bounded-direct, and 1.495/1.543 against unbounded Caffeine. At 4 KiB, all three contrasts favor Caffeine (default 1.143/1.166; direct 1.068/1.083; unbounded 1.249/1.270). Chronicle anchors are shared across the three within-block policy contrasts: they are dependent comparisons. Unbounded changes capacity contract and layout; it is a sensitivity, not a fair replacement for a requirement to enforce a size bound.

The separate JMH regime gives mixed 512 B service p99.9 ordering at both pressures. At 1 KiB it favors Caffeine at both pressures (ratios 1.607–2.410), as does 4 KiB (1.100–1.266). JMH changes caller, sparse sampling, consumption and iteration/reset boundaries together. This shows harness-regime sensitivity; it does not isolate a causal timer-overhead effect. JMH records samples, not every completed operation. Actual collection is 14 one-second warmup iterations (last two used diagnostically as conditioning) and eight one-second measurement iterations. The common controller's unused generic `measureMs=30000` field is preserved and explicitly clarified in the retained cohort notes; it is not the JMH measurement duration.

### The response reversal depends on logical pattern

| Equal-array 4 KiB baseline pattern | Caffeine response p99.9, paired forks | Chronicle response p99.9, paired forks | Ch/C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repeated-short | 25.599 / 23.391 µs | 224.895 / 171.263 µs | 8.785 / 7.322 |
| Uniform-long | 8.626 / 8.454 ms | 171.775 / 395.519 µs | 0.01991 / 0.04678 |

Both arms allocate exactly 1,000,003 long-key slots and boolean flags: 9,000,027 payload bytes, 950,003 reads and 50,000 writes. Maximum observed humongous-region counts match across patterns within each backend (Caffeine 10; Chronicle 9). Repeated-short preserves 1,939 distinct updated keys; uniform-long updates 25,532. Two final flag corrections preserve the common read/write totals. The temporary short source is not retained. One repeated-short Caffeine fork carries a compiler warning; the other pair and both uniform-long pairs are clean.

Raw trace-array geometry alone is therefore insufficient to explain the ranking. Read-set coverage, ordering and allocation/object lifetime still change together. We cannot attribute the result uniquely to the mutation set, locality, GC or a specific maintenance action. This control is baseline response, not a new pressure threshold.

### Why GC-heavy service p99.9 can remain small

One 4 KiB / 336 MiB Caffeine saturated fork completes 28,508,698 operations with 624 GC pauses, including 312 full GCs. GC overlaps 39.3595% of measured wall time. Its large pauses occur inside only 312 timed calls: **0.0010944%** of operations. Service p99.9 is 8,367 ns, p99.99 is 19,887 ns, and p99.999 is 35.783 ms. Chronicle p99.999 is 1.465 ms. Wall-time share and the fraction of affected operations have different denominators. The fact that every large pause in this particular fork overlaps service does not generalize to every fork; some other pauses occur between timed calls.

Extreme-tail ranking is nonmonotonic: at 4 KiB / 240, 272, 304 and 336 MiB the Caffeine/Chronicle service p99.999 values are approximately 0.853/1.479, 2.687/1.426, 1.058/1.427 and 35.783/1.465 ms. Neither a single pressure threshold nor distribution-wide superiority follows. Full curves and >100 µs, >1 ms and >10 ms probabilities accompany every exported distribution; HDR bin bounds describe quantization, not statistical uncertainty.

## Worth-first stopping and reproducibility

The 8 KiB pilot's frozen rule required a credible improving pressure direction before expansion, or a sufficiently strong baseline win for payload confirmation. Neither triggered. With 32,768 residents, 16 KiB payload alone consumes 512 MiB and 32 KiB consumes 1 GiB, exceeding the fixed heap once Caffeine metadata is included. These infeasible cells were not launched. The completed study is the full declared bounded sequence, not a Cartesian product of all possible workloads.

The portable [JSON](followup-data-20261117.json) has SHA-256 `8d6468c0b7615f0afe03841b8992cd6ea8a0e14ca2b4f00da383e4aa36222aef`: 184 research forks, 96 paired contrasts and 58 point groups. The [independent verifier](portable-verification-20261117.json) passes 704 distributions, 3,520 quantiles, 160 exact histogram unions, 480 threshold checks, 160 mixture checks, 1,840 paired ratios and 1,120 point conclusions. These portable curves cover workload forks; raw audits also check controls. [Seven figures](FIGURES_20261117.md) have pinned render dependencies and output hashes. Clean-directory reproduction matches all 14 SVG/PNG outputs without raw archives. A separate JAR-only fresh-source compile and four Caffeine API/capacity checks pass. These are build/data checks, not independent performance replication or human peer review.

All eight new cohorts use one host/boot, Intel Core i7-8750H, OpenJDK 21.0.13, G1, 512 MiB heap, one worker and common 32 MiB/s churn. The 10 November cohort used Java 21.0.12.1 and remains separate. Source-matched audits verify 72/79/85/89-file runtime groups. Chronicle analytics is disabled; tiering policy is logged. These are in-memory comparisons, with no storage-open claim. The old frozen `docs/preprint/paper-data.json` remains byte-identical.
