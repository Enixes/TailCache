# Final systems-research and artifact review

**17 November 2026. Agent-assisted technical assessment, not independent human peer review.** Scope: eight completed 17 November exploratory cohorts, their code/audits, portable package, supplementary figures and revised narrative. The original unconditional experiment remains NO_GO. [Results](RESULTS_20261117.md) state the positive, negative and contradictory findings together.

## Assessment

The bounded experimental scope is scientifically reportable as exploratory evidence. It does **not** support a general ordinary-service crossover or a trace-invariant scheduled-response pressure threshold. Its stronger contribution is evidence that complete cache configuration, logical trace pattern, observer regime and latency denominator can change the interpretation. The manuscript now states that outcome directly. No additional benchmark is required to finish this scope; independent replication and human contribution/novelty review remain valuable publication work.

## Remaining research and release limits

One host/boot and few paired forks; adaptive sequential experiment choices; compiler/CPU/churn warnings; periodic traces; in-process pacing rather than an external client; single-byte value consumption rather than formal DCE proof; implementation/configuration differences rather than a heap-placement intervention; sampled RSS rather than total-memory attribution; rare samples and correlated queued responses. The equal-array test does not separate locality, mutation set, ordering or object lifetime. No significance, universal threshold, workload-wide superiority or production SLA is claimed.

Human authors still need to judge contribution/novelty, verify related-work coverage for the chosen venue, revise/type-set the manuscript, agree names/roles/disclosure/licensing, and approve a selected raw-evidence/public artifact release and deposit. The hosted working explorer is authenticated; it is not a public paper or formal publication. Benchmark completion and reproducibility checks do not substitute for these decisions.
