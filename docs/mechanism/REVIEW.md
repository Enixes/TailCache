## Implementation and fairness

New isolated classes preserve legacy measured bytes. Full-set generation matches the earlier continuous stream. Only writes are routed into the update-set prefix; read keys, write flags, arrival rate, resident count and fresh-array sizes/counts stay matched within seed. Values encode actual key/version and are immutable after insertion. Independent replay verifies logical counts and final mutation-version digests; every resident is checked. Chronicle has no retained payload/finite-trace bank. Ordinary get materialization remains part of its contract; no reuse result substitutes for it.

Three functional JVMs plus two source-matched native qualification controls pass. Six schedule tests enforce within-treatment balance, repeat reversal and fresh headroom confirmation. The launcher alias error happened before any JVM started and is retained as a prelaunch failure; it is not an excluded research result. Reporting prototypes stayed outside the frozen measured-source set until the sequence exited0 and its native scope became inactive. A prototype percentile-rank bug was caught by comparison with pinned HDR semantics and corrected before delivery; raw measurements were unchanged.

All five histograms conserve operations and exact read/update unions, including genuinely empty update channels at 0%. Sparse>=100 µs events use fixed capacity and phase-relative operation indices; no overflow or log drop is accepted silently. Source/runtime/JDK/commands, actual heap, policy/tiering, analytics disablement, physical-host/RSS/power/storage and GC evidence are retained. The benchmark lock prevents duplicate native jobs; completed slots are preserved on resume. Deadline and JVM ceilings bound collection. No Gradle/package/runtime changes occurred during cohorts.

The final review also caught a controller metadata error: frozen qualification/logging protocols each say `functionalValidationJvms=3` and `functionalReceiptReused=false`, although the same one-functional/two-smoke setup was executed once and reused. Their copied receipt hashes are identical, and archived build/smoke branches return after checking retained receipts. The scope total is **three**, not six or a per-cohort sum. Frozen protocols remain unchanged; the final evidence receipt records unique receipt identities and this clarification. The controller description is corrected after collection for future distinct cohorts. No research timing/value/result changes, reruns or exclusions follow from this metadata correction.

## Quality and remaining confounds

Forty-two of 64 forks have49 warning instances:31 zswpin, one zswpout, nine compiler, seven outside-CPU and one thermal-throttle. Fourteen Caffeine forks have measured evacuation failures; Chronicle has none. No measured full GC occurs. These are retained outcomes, not exclusions. Important contrasts have warning-free observations, but that does not certify the whole study as externally quiet or steady-state neutral.

The historical half-window occupancy rule compared four early-cycle observations with two mixed collections. It failed as declared but cannot establish physical nonstationarity in every fork. The manuscript now discloses this distinction and the old order imbalance. New constrained schedules improve order control; only two seeds/repeats still limit population inference. Priming bundles invocation/phase-allocation effects with JIT profiles. Logging selection has host warnings and is development. Update-set repeats are repeated seeds, not fresh holdouts. The1024 MiB comparison changes memory capacity/GC behavior, not one isolated cause. Internal pacing and background churn remain synthetic; an independent arrival observer is still missing.

## Publication stopping point

This scope is sufficient to revise the bounded exploratory manuscript; it is not a foolproof general solution. The highest-value next work is independent expert/contribution feedback, selected clean independent-host/external-client replication and an author-approved citable artifact/manuscript release. More same-host midpoint searching is not required to report these results. A collector/API/skew experiment needs its own prediction, scope and worth decision; no automatic expansion remains from this plan.

Deployment, browser/DOM coverage and recovery shutdown are recorded in the current operational/QA receipts. Real-browser signoff must not be inferred from a successful source hash, synthetic DOM check or scientific-figure inspection. No journal submission, author approval or public preprint deposit is implied.
