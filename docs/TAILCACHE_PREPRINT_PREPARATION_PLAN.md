# Preprint preparation: implementation plan

Prepared 11 November 2026 before editing the paper package. This unit uses retained evidence only. No collection, gate change, submission or public manuscript release is scheduled.

## Deliverables and order

1. Inspect the audited focused and midpoint datasets and freeze their hashes as paper inputs. Keep runtime, workload, seed and API-contract cohorts separate.
2. Create a claim-to-evidence table, with a machine-readable counterpart, exact input pointers, warning coverage and limits. Reconcile historical cliff claims without rewriting archived evidence.
3. Produce a consistent static scientific figure set: fresh response replication and paired ratios; historical saturated service versus scheduled response; per-fork survival/threshold evidence; allocation/GC; heap versus RSS; secondary percentile/seed sensitivity. No pooled histograms, inferred precise thresholds or confidence intervals from three forks.
4. Verify primary literature and write the related-work and methods sections in an editable manuscript skeleton. Describe this as an exploratory, instrumented, one-host study; leave authorship and final release to author review.
5. Add a small offline reproduction path and export selected derived inputs so paper figures can be regenerated from a clean checkout without collecting data or accessing the protected explorer. Raw-evidence release and clean full-harness reproduction remain Task 15 work.
6. Update current planning notes to mark the midpoint/seed work complete while preserving dated historical decisions.
7. Verify generated numerical claims against the audited JSON/CSV inputs, inspect rendered figures, check cross-references and record a researcher review with remaining prepublication work. This is agent review, not independent human peer review.

## Completion gates for this unit

- Every headline has an evidence row and explicit scope; mixed 104 MiB and rare-tail support remain visible.
- Figure data are per-fork, self-contained, hashed and regenerable with documented commands; source archives and retained results are unchanged.
- No comparison silently mixes source groups, seeds, service/response experiments or ownership contracts.
- Captions distinguish ranges, histogram-resolution bounds and inferential uncertainty; zero observed exceedances are not zero risk.
- Primary literature citations are checked and a novelty comparison is recorded.
- Methods/related work and an outline exist; the manuscript is not labelled publication-ready or submitted.

Remaining beyond this unit: complete manuscript prose, author/contributor agreement, independent human feedback, raw-evidence packaging/backup, clean harness reproduction, final technical audit and author-approved release before the application deadline.
