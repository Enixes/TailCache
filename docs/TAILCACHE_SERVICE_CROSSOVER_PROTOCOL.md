# Frozen bounded service protocol — 16 November 2026

This operational specification precedes performance collection. The follow-up is a separate exploratory cohort. Prior paper inputs, source/runtime archives, rejected forks and the protected presentation remain preserved. Code and this protocol are hash-bound in each attempt before any workload JVM is launched.

## Workload and endpoints

- Primary: ordinary-get **combined 95/5 saturated-service p99.9**. Secondary: combined p99. Read/update service, p99.99/p99.999, inter-completion gap, throughput, allocation, RSS and GC are diagnostics. No switch of primary metric after observations.
- One cache worker, 32,768 residents, G1 `Xms=Xmx=512 MiB`, immutable freshly generated writes, common offered churn 32 MiB/s with 4 KiB objects and a 128-slot ring. No eviction, persistence or reuse arm. Chronicle analytics disabled and tiering/capacity logged.
- One pre-generated uniform trace of **1,000,003 operations**, exactly 950,003 reads and 50,000 updates, seed 24301. Report actual distinct accessed/read/update keys and fingerprint. Primitive trace-array payload is 9,000,027 bytes, excluding headers; this increased common retained footprint is not cache payload. Setup/final scans visit all residents. Cursor resets at phase boundaries.
- Timed service contains trace traversal, write generation, access and value consumption. Classification and all histogram recording occur after the completion timestamp. Added recording can affect later execution and is not presumed neutral. Report exact union of read/update distributions and count conservation.
- Per workload JVM: 12 s warmup, 2 s conditioning, 30 s measurement. Timeout 100 s includes setup/export/verification; an incomplete or failed run is retained as an outcome. No new process replaces the same slot. No synthetic latency correction or timing-floor subtraction.

## Controls and functional validation

Two bounded functional-check JVMs (preliminary and final) validate the new path and both backends; they are not research data. Four declared controls each use 12/2/4 s phases: timer floor, timer floor with a single 20 ms injected stall, fresh-supply floor at 256 B and at 4 KiB. They use 64 residents, no ballast/churn and the same longer trace/recorder. Injected stall must appear in service maximum at >=10 ms. Controls check values, conservation, floor and pause visibility; they do not prove observer neutrality. Record control warnings instead of hiding them.

## Sequence and adaptive decisions

1. **Screen: 20 workload JVMs.** Payloads 256/512/1024/2048/4096 B, ballast 0/208 MiB, one independent paired fork per cell. Shuffle the ten cells with seed 24301 and alternate backend order. Preserve every cell, including contrary, failed and warning-bearing observations. This is discovery only.
2. **Choose at most one payload.** Among payloads with complete valid screen pairs at both points and baseline Chronicle/Caffeine p99.9 ratio >=1, prioritize an existing meaningful pressure bracket, then lowest 208-MiB ratio, then smallest high/baseline ratio, then smallest payload. A meaningful descriptive pressure bracket has lower-ballast ratio >=1.10 and higher-ballast ratio <=0.90. These are predeclared practical margins, not significance bounds. If none is eligible, skip pressure probes.
3. **At most four new paired pressure probes: eight JVMs.** Without a bracket, target Caffeine's observed median post-GC heap occupancy at 80%, 90%, 95%, 97% in successive probes. Estimate new common ballast as `floor_to_4MiB(current_ballast + (target - measured_occupancy)*512)`. Do not equalize total occupancy between backends. Cap ballast at `floor_to_4MiB(512 - resident_payload_MiB - trace_array_payload_MiB - 24)`. The 24 MiB reserve is a bounded search policy, not a measured overhead claim. Stop if a previous selected-payload pair failed, occupancy is unavailable, or the next point cannot increase ballast within the cap. Calibration counts within this probe budget.
4. If a meaningful pressure bracket exists, allow **one** rounded 4 MiB midpoint probe, then stop probing regardless of its result. Choose the narrowest observed meaningful bracket; tie-break by smaller lower ballast. No assumption of monotonicity, repeated bisection until success or interpolation to an exact point.
5. **Select fresh confirmation before collecting it.** Prefer the selected payload's pressure bracket. Otherwise inspect screen payloads in ascending order at common ballast 0 first, then 208 MiB. Choose the first adjacent tested-payload pair with smaller-payload ratio <=0.90 and larger-payload ratio >=1.10. Declare this a **payload-ranking bracket**, not a pressure crossover. Failed cells are not bridged to make an adjacent-payload claim. If neither exists, stop with a descriptive map/no bracket.
6. **Confirmation ceiling: 16 new JVMs.** At each selected endpoint collect three new paired forks with seed 24301, shuffled endpoint/block order with seed 24302 and alternating backend order. One additional pair per endpoint uses held-out seed 24302 and remains separate. Confirmation does not pool discovery forks or seeds. A repeated endpoint direction requires all three primary pairs to agree and their median to meet the practical margin; report every ratio and holdout direction. Contrary/quality-limited confirmation stays mixed or conditional, never silently replaced.

Absolute ceiling: **44 workload JVMs**, four control JVMs and two separately labelled bounded functional JVMs. No hidden calibration sweep. Stop earlier when no further stage is justified. Completed valid slots are checksum-verified on resume. An interrupted unresolved slot stops for explicit adjudication rather than being duplicated; its attempt still counts toward the ceiling.

## Host, quality and evidence

Use the existing exclusive native lock and user-owned accounting scope. Before new slots require external AC power, at least 1 GiB available RAM, at least 512 MiB free disk, unchanged source/runtime hashes and no new swap-in/out since controller readiness. A failed readiness condition preserves completed slots for resume. One-second host and process-status observations retain actual JVM cgroup membership, thermal/power/CPU/paging records and measured-window sampled RSS.

Keep compiler activity >80 ms, achieved/dropped churn demand, native host reasons and all pressure failures visible. A complete value-correct distribution is available for warning-inclusive descriptive analysis; warning-free coverage is shown separately. Neither external warnings nor a successful run grant unconditional qualification. Do not remove slow observations or use a clean subset to erase contrary pairs. Resource and phase counters must reconcile with raw files. Reject corrupted counts/source identity and fail functional controls before dependent workloads.

Before calling the cohort complete, independently decode all available HDRs using pinned HdrHistogram 2.2.2, compare all bins and quantiles, reconcile raw GC intervals, export per-pair tables with conclusions and archive inventories, and write a research review. Collection completion alone is not research completion. An OOM or timeout is a feasibility boundary, not a latency win.
