## Observed failure, before implementation

Sequence native-final-20261105-001 passed the controlled CPU probe (inside/outside upper bounds 4.40%/28.59%), 120 consecutive quiet seconds, all 14 functional host/mechanical blocks, both two-window pause checks, and all eight JMH compiler/host blocks. Its first every-operation fork then failed the unchanged compiler limit: 11/0/0/0/0/0/0/1 ms across measurements, against 10 ms per iteration. The full sequence stopped, leaving seven every-operation, pressure and independent-floor stages uncollected. Nothing from this failed sequence enters calibration or primary endpoints.

Raw XML gives a concrete cause. ApplicationChurn.allocate suffered an unstable_if trap at bytecode 142 at JVM uptime 91.692 s, in late warmup. javap maps this to the inner batch's `&& running` stop check. The C2 recompilation ran at 96.726–96.738 s, overlapping the first measured interval 96.715868–97.715868 s. Its 12 ms task wall time is corroborating timing, not compiler CPU time; the worker's actual compiler-time counter is the rejecting 11 ms. No measurement-phase workload-branch trap was found before the final window ended. Ordinary late stop timing exposed a rare inner-loop branch after most warmups had completed.

## Planned repair

Keep the outer volatile running check and finish each already admitted bounded batch, capped at 256 arrays, before checking stop again. Remove only the inner per-object stop condition. No new allocation is admitted after the outer check observes stop; no catch-up backlog is replayed. The existing 10-second join bound and worker-error propagation remain. A stop may finish the current batch, at most 1 MiB at the campaign's 4096-byte object size; retained ring capacity stays 128 objects. The interval's churn duration and allocated-byte totals include the completed batch and join, as they already included stop teardown. Both latency channels use the same allocator and continue to require achieved churn within ±10%, zero dropped demand and JVM allocation corroboration.

This defines stop at a bounded work unit rather than racing each object in that unit. It changes auxiliary allocation timing and therefore measurement source identity. Keep every compiler/host/exposure limit and the candidate 96 warmups unchanged. Repeat full source-matched qualification in a new sequence native-final-20261105-002. Preserve sequence 001 as rejected; no unchanged retry or pooling is authorized.
